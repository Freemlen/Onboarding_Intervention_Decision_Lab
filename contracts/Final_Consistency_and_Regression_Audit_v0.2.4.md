Counting distinct lifecycles avoids double-counting state notifications or the derived guardrail event itself. ### R2 — shared predicate, no hidden subtype distribution or causal feedback

For eligible paths, every member of the one existing protection composite has the same communication restriction interpretation, so no unobserved subtype must be chosen. Explicit audit stop/restriction fields project already required audit evidence and are known null for generated configurations, not newly sampled processes. Equality is resolved before the reminder or contact start. As-of successful settlement uses only dispositions by the evaluation time, rather than treating a future reversal as already observed. Its mandatory estimate/variance/SE/CI bounds serialize as numeric 0, p-value as 1, statistic as null, and status as ZERO_SE_ZERO_ESTIMATE. Permutations and arm swaps do not change that result.
